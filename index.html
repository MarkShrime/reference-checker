How to read the results. Critical = the reference may be fabricated or points to the wrong work (not found, wrong title, wrong authors, wrong DOI or ISBN, dead web link, year off by more than one). Minor = the work exists but a detail differs (journal name/abbreviation, publisher, volume, pages, year off by one, web page content that no longer matches the citation). Journal articles are verified via Crossref and PubMed, books via Google Books and Open Library, and website references by loading the cited page (via a public relay, with the Internet Archive as backup). Automated matching isn't perfect — always double-check flagged items by opening the linked record.
